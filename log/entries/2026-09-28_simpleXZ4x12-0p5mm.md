# 2026-09-28 — simpleXZ 4x1x12 short gate

## Question

Can shifting more of the 48 static partitions into the transverse z direction
reduce the moving-AMR imbalance without sacrificing actual runtime?

## Controlled change

Reference:
`simpleXZ (8 1 6)`, accepted at the 0.5 mm gate.

Probe:
`simpleXZ (4 1 12)`.

All physics, mesh, solver controls, track length and hardware remained fixed.

## Result

Fusion-zone geometry was unchanged at reported resolution:

```text
W 435.187 -> 435.187 um
D 60.9995 -> 60.9995 um
L 685.187 -> 685.187 um
V +0.204%
```

Load balance improved:

```text
max/mean imbalance 1.683 -> 1.401
local cells         37,620 / 56,051
imbalance change    -16.8%
```

However performance regressed:

```text
ClockTime              4260 -> 5672 s   (+33.1%)
final-interval wall    2979 -> 3695 s   (+24.1%)
pressure wall          1059 -> 1229 s   (+16.1%)
thermal wall            593 ->  670 s   (+12.9%)
```

The probe used 3264 final-interval steps versus 2671 for the 8x1x6 reference
(+22.2%). Even after normalising by step count, wall cost increased slightly:

```text
8x1x6 wall/step   1.1153 s
4x1x12 wall/step  1.1322 s
                 +1.5%
```

Beam accounting, continuity and termination were normal. There was one
interface thermal-cap hit, not enough to change the decision.

## Interpretation

The 4x1x12 result exposes a real tradeoff:

- more transverse partitions improve cell-count balance;
- the extra partition interfaces/communication do not improve per-step cost;
- the changed floating-point/partition trajectory also generated more time
  steps, making total runtime substantially worse.

Therefore cell-count imbalance alone cannot be the optimisation target.

The static-layout search has now produced both sides of the tradeoff:

- 8x1x6: much faster at 0.5 mm, but imbalance grows strongly by 1 mm;
- 4x1x12: better 0.5 mm balance, but slower in both total and per-step terms.

## Decision

**REJECT 4x1x12 for 1 mm promotion.**

Do not continue enumerating static surface-plane layouts unless a new physical
or algorithmic argument justifies one.

## Next gate

Move to a checkpoint redistribution workflow.

The intended architecture is:

```text
initial simpleXZ 8x1x6
        ↓
moving AMR develops
        ↓
write/checkpoint at a controlled time
        ↓
redistribute existing decomposed mesh + fields with Scotch
        ↓
verify dynamicRefineFvMesh state and cumulative fields
        ↓
resume solver
```

First build a short redistribution smoke test. It must prove restart fidelity
before any full-track performance claims.
